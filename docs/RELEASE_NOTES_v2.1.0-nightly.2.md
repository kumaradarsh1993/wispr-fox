# wispr-fox v2.1.0-nightly.2

Polish pass on the minimal avatars, plus a new one.

## Wave bar — redesigned

Smaller, calmer, and much more Apple. The pill is now roughly Clippy-sized,
with a soft **gray translucent** background and slim **white** bars (no more
orange). The bars stay gently centered and never stretch to the edges — they
top out well inside the pill. It scales with the S/M/L slider like every other
avatar.

The bars now also *mean* something:

- **Listening** — a live waveform that moves with your voice.
- **Transcribing** — a soft shimmer that ripples across the bars.
- **Done** — a one-second green breathe.
- **Error** — three quick red blinks.

Its default spot moved down from the very top edge to a more natural
**top-center, about 10% below the top** of the screen.

## New: Siri Orb avatar

A tiny multicolor orb — think Siri. It drifts through its gradient at rest,
**blooms and brightens with your voice** while listening, swirls faster while
transcribing, flashes a green ring on success, and shudders red on an error.
No text, no bubbles. It pops up on the **right-hand side** by default, and like
every avatar it's draggable, scales with the slider, and has **Reset position**
in its right-click menu.

*Nightly build — try the two minimal avatars and tell the fox what to tune.*
