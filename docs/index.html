Desktop dictation that stays yours

Press a key. Talk. Get text.

wispr-fox is a free, open-source dictation app for Windows and macOS. Hold a hotkey, speak naturally, and drop text into Slack, Gmail, Word, terminals, or any focused field.

Choose a mode to see how the same spoken thought lands in the app you are writing in.

Made for everyday writing.

Works anywhere

Use global hotkeys to insert text into the app you are already using. No special editor required.

Three useful modes

Capture verbatim speech, clean punctuation and paragraphs, or turn a spoken brief into a polished draft.

Bring your own key

Use Groq for Whisper transcription and optional Gemini or Groq models for cleanup and drafting.

No account. No telemetry.

Keys in OS keychain

API keys are stored through Windows Credential Manager or macOS Keychain.

Recordings stay local

Audio history lives on your machine with retention limits you control.
