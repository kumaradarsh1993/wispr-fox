# wispr-fox v3.2.0

> Stable release · 11 August 2026

This release brings the fox-in-the-field redesign and personal voice insights
from the v3.2 nightlies to the stable channel.

## A calmer, clearer desktop

- The oversized settings-heavy sidebar is now a focused navigation rail, with
  everyday voice controls grouped into one compact card.
- Settings is organised around Voice, AI engines, Writing, Avatar, App & data,
  Account, and Advanced, and adapts cleanly to compact windows.
- History uses clearer expand controls, actions, search, card hierarchy, and
  responsive layouts while keeping the warm field-and-paper character.

## A better first-run journey

- Onboarding now tells one consistent fox-in-the-field story across Welcome,
  Voice, Try it, and Sync.
- Named progress steps, accessible controls, reliable skip state, and correct
  provider-key detection make setup easier to understand and harder to get
  stuck in.

## Your recent voice signature

- Insights now describes patterns in your retained raw microphone dictation:
  pace and consistency, session and sentence shape, question share, vocabulary
  breadth, discourse markers, and adjacent repeated words.
- Analysis stays on-device and deliberately excludes uploads, meeting speakers,
  and AI-transformed text so it reflects your own recent dictation.
- Minimum-sample safeguards and plain-language limitations avoid making claims
  about accent, pronunciation, or vocal fluency from transcript text alone.

## Avatar and interface polish

- Avatar names and picker metadata are consistent across the shell, Settings,
  and floater menu.
- Fox, Clippo, Blacky, Wavy, and Siri have clearer picker art, while Mochi &
  Marmalade joins the validated animated roster.
- Shared typography, field colors, surfaces, focus treatment, motion, and
  reduced-motion behavior make the redesigned areas feel like one application.
