# wispr-fox 3.6.0

**Meetings and calls get their own page.**

## What's new

- **Voice note or Conversation.** Every upload asks which one it is. A conversation goes to a new Conversations page and stays off Home.
- **Two transcripts per conversation.** One labels who said what. The other has more accurate wording. Both are kept, and meeting notes are written from both.
- **Name your speakers once.** The names carry into the transcript, the notes, copied text and the export, and they sync to your other devices.
- **Retry one step at a time.** If one step fails, such as the notes, you retry just that step. The parts that worked are kept.
- **Export for AI assistants.** One Markdown file with both transcripts, ready to paste into Claude or ChatGPT.

## Fixed

- **Mac:** no more repeated Keychain password prompts on launch. After an update you're asked at most once.
