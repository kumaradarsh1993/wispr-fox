# wispr-fox v2.1.0-nightly.4

Avatar housekeeping and warmer comings-and-goings.

## Khaumani & Indy retired

The hand-drawn two-cat avatar is gone. If you had it selected, you're
automatically moved to **Oru & Gujia** — the same two-cat idea, drawn
properly. One less so-so option in the picker.

## Every avatar now arrives and leaves in its own style

- **Characters** (fox, cats, Clippy…) pop up from the ground with a
  little overshoot, and shrink away when they go.
- The **Wave bar** stretches open from a sliver while its bars pop up
  left to right, and collapses back to a sliver on exit.
- The **Siri Orb** blooms open with a twist, and spins away.

These play on first launch, on every appear/disappear in the
"While dictating" visibility mode, and — new — **when you quit the app**:
the tray's Quit now gives the avatar a beat to wave goodbye before the
process exits. The avatar SDK documents the hook so future avatar packs
can define their own entrances.

## Wave bar polish

- Bars get a whisper of glow while listening.
- Success is now a green pop that ripples across the bars left to right
  instead of a flat color change.
