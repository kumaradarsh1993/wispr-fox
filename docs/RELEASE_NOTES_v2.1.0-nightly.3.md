# wispr-fox v2.1.0-nightly.3

A do-over on both minimal avatars, based on your feedback.

## Wave bar — the bars finally dance

The pill no longer scrolls a waveform history across all the bars. Instead
there are **12 individual bars** that rest as tiny dots and **expand and
contract independently** with your voice — each bar has its own slightly
different rhythm, so the pill moves like Wispr Flow's, not like a ticker
tape. Louder speech pushes the bars taller (never past ~68% of the pill),
quiet moments let them fall back toward dots.

The pill itself is also a touch smaller and lighter — 120×32 with a softer
shadow — so it sits on screen more quietly.

- **Listening** — bars dance with your voice, taller in the middle.
- **Transcribing** — a soft pulse travels left-to-right on a loop.
- **Done** — a one-second green breathe.
- **Error** — three quick red blinks.

## Siri Orb — rebuilt from scratch

The flat color-wheel is gone. The orb is now a proper **fluid, glassy
sphere**: six color fields (Siri pink, blue, purple over a near-white
heart) rotate at different speeds — some counter-rotating — and melt into
each other, so the colors genuinely swirl and blob the way Apple's orb
does. A glass highlight on top gives it the 3D ball feel.

It still blooms with your voice while listening, swirls faster while
transcribing, flashes a green ring on success, and shudders red on error.

*Nightly build — install, flip between Wave bar and Siri Orb, and tell the
fox what to tune.*
